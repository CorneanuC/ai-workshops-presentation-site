# Workshop 15: AI Output Verification Lab

Duration: 60 minutes

Audience: All factory departments

## Purpose

Teach employees to challenge AI output and catch errors, assumptions, bias, missing facts, and risky wording.

## 60-Minute Agenda

- 0-5 min: Explain why AI output must be verified.
- 5-15 min: Find invented facts.
- 15-25 min: Check numbers, dates, and names.
- 25-35 min: Check risky commitments.
- 35-45 min: Check bias and tone.
- 45-55 min: Create verification checklist.
- 55-60 min: Save checklist.

## Exercise 1: Find Invented Facts

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Review this AI-generated answer. Identify any statement that is not directly supported by the input. Mark each as supported, unsupported, or needs verification.
```

Examples:

- HR: AI adds a training date that was not provided.
- Customer Service: AI promises compensation not approved.
- Finance: AI assumes invoice approval without evidence.
- Quality: AI invents a root cause.
- Maintenance: AI assumes machine failure reason.

Output: unsupported claims table.

## Exercise 2: Check Numbers, Dates, and Names

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Compare the AI output against the original input. Check all numbers, dates, names, departments, invoice references, line numbers, and deadlines.
```

Output: accuracy check table.

## Exercise 3: Check Risky Commitments

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Review this message for commitments, promises, decisions, approvals, legal language, financial implications, or policy statements that require approval.
```

Output: risk and approval list.

## Exercise 4: Check Bias, Tone, and Fairness

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Review this text for biased, blaming, unfair, unclear, or overly emotional wording. Suggest neutral alternatives.
```

Output: neutral wording improvements.

## Exercise 5: Create a Verification Checklist

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Build a checklist every employee can use before relying on AI output.

Checklist sections:

- facts supported by input
- numbers unchanged
- no invented dates or owners
- no unauthorized promises
- no confidential data exposed
- tone is professional
- human approval step is clear

Output: department AI verification checklist.
