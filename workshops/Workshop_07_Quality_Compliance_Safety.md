# Workshop 07: AI for Quality, Compliance, and Safety

Duration: 60 minutes

Audience: HR, Clients/Customer Service, Finance

## Purpose

Teach teams to use AI for clearer checks, stronger documentation, and better preparation for audits or reviews.

## 60-Minute Agenda

- 0-5 min: Explain why accuracy and evidence matter.
- 5-55 min: Five practical exercises.
- 55-60 min: Save review prompts and checklist items.

## Exercise 1: Create a Compliance Checklist

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Create a checklist for reviewing this process. Include required evidence, owner, frequency, and common errors.
```

HR:
- Training completion compliance.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Customer complaint closure quality.

Finance:
- Invoice approval compliance.

Output: Compliance checklist.

## Exercise 2: Improve an Incident or Issue Report

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Review this issue report. Improve clarity and identify missing facts. Do not invent causes.
```

HR:
- Missed training incident.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Escalated customer complaint.

Finance:
- Payment delay issue.

Output: Improved issue report and missing facts.

## Exercise 3: Root Cause Thinking

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Use 5 Whys thinking on this issue. Clearly separate confirmed facts from possible causes.
```

HR:
- Low training attendance.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Repeated complaint type.

Finance:
- Recurring invoice errors.

Output: 5 Whys draft with verification needs.

## Exercise 4: Audit Preparation

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Prepare an audit readiness list for this area. Include documents needed, questions auditors may ask, and evidence to prepare.
```

HR:
- Training records.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Complaint response records.

Finance:
- Approval and payment records.

Output: Audit readiness list.

## Exercise 5: Risk Wording Review

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Rewrite these risks so they are specific, measurable, and easy to understand. Include possible controls.
```

HR:
- Risk of incomplete training records.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Risk of inconsistent customer responses.

Finance:
- Risk of incorrect payments.

Output: Risk and control table.
