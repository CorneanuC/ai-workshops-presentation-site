# Recommended Factory Program Structure

## Recommendation

Use the existing 10 workshops as the core program, then add 7 practical factory accelerator workshops.

This creates two possible offers:

1. Core program: 10 workshops for AI literacy, safe use, prompting, document review, communication, data, and adoption.
2. Factory accelerator program: 17 workshops for faster business value across Production, Quality, Maintenance, Logistics, HR, Customer Service, Finance, Procurement, and Management.

## Why Add the 7 Workshops

The 10 core workshops teach the foundation. The 7 additional workshops create immediate factory value because participants work on real department artifacts:

- SOPs
- shift handovers
- daily reports
- nonconformity reports
- customer complaints
- invoice issues
- KPI summaries
- audit checklists
- training materials
- department prompt libraries

## Best Format

Each workshop should stay 60 minutes:

- 5 min: introduce one practical AI workflow
- 10 min: facilitator demo using a factory example
- 30 min: participants work on their own department task
- 10 min: peer review and accuracy check
- 5 min: save the output into the department library

## Suggested Sequence

### Option A: Fast Pilot, 5 Workshops

Use this if the factory wants value quickly.

1. AI Use-Case Discovery Sprint
2. Prompt-a-thon: Build Your Department Prompt Library
3. AI for SOPs, Work Instructions, and Training Materials
4. AI Output Verification Lab
5. AI Champions and Adoption Lab

### Option B: Full Factory Program, 17 Workshops

Use this if the factory wants full adoption across departments.

1. AI Foundations for Non-Technical Teams
2. Prompting Skills
3. AI for Emails, Reports, and Daily Communication
4. AI for Document Accuracy and Double-Checking
5. AI for HR and People Operations
6. AI for Operations and Productivity
7. AI for Quality, Compliance, and Safety
8. AI for Excel, Data, and Decision Support
9. Safe and Responsible AI Use
10. Department AI Use-Case Lab
11. AI Use-Case Discovery Sprint
12. Prompt-a-thon: Build Your Department Prompt Library
13. AI for SOPs, Work Instructions, and Training Materials
14. AI for Shift Handover, Daily Reports, and Issue Summaries
15. AI Output Verification Lab
16. AI for Excel, KPIs, and Management Summaries
17. AI Champions and Adoption Lab

## Rule for Every Workshop

Every workshop must produce at least one reusable artifact:

- prompt
- checklist
- template
- SOP draft
- report format
- data summary
- workflow
- safety rule
- pilot plan
