# Practical Deliverables Build Plan

This plan shows how to build the program deliverables step by step across the 10 core workshops and the 7 optional factory accelerator workshops.

## Deliverables

By the end of the program, the company should have:

1. AI Prompt Library by Department
2. Document Review Checklist
3. Safe AI Usage Policy
4. Department Use-Case Map
5. Before/After Productivity Examples
6. AI Champions Group

## Suggested Final Structure for Each Deliverable

### 1. AI Prompt Library by Department

Sections:
- HR prompts
- Clients/Customer Service prompts
- Finance prompts
- General document review prompts
- Data and Excel prompts
- Safety and verification prompts

Minimum content:
- 5 final prompts per department
- 5 shared prompts
- Usage notes and risk warnings

### 2. Document Review Checklist

Sections:
- Grammar and clarity
- Missing information
- Contradictions
- Numbers, names, and dates
- Policy or process consistency
- Human verification log

Minimum content:
- Shared checklist
- Department-specific checklist items
- Final approval rule

### 3. Safe AI Usage Policy

Sections:
- Purpose
- Approved uses
- Restricted uses
- Prohibited data
- Green/yellow/red classification
- Human accountability
- Verification requirements
- Department-specific rules

Minimum content:
- 10 company-level rules
- 5 rules per department
- Examples of safe and unsafe use

### 4. Department Use-Case Map

Sections:
- Use case name
- Department
- Problem solved
- Input
- AI output
- Human review step
- Risk level
- Owner
- Success measure

Minimum content:
- 3 priority use cases per department
- 1 workflow per department ready for pilot

### 5. Before/After Productivity Examples

Sections:
- Current process
- AI-assisted process
- Time before
- Time after
- Quality improvement
- Remaining risks
- Human control

Minimum content:
- 2 examples per department
- 1 quantified improvement target per example

### 6. AI Champions Group

Sections:
- Champion role
- Selection criteria
- Responsibilities
- Monthly meeting format
- Feedback collection
- Prompt library ownership
- Safety escalation path

Minimum content:
- 1 champion per department
- 1 backup champion per department
- Monthly review rhythm

## 30-Day Post-Program Implementation Plan

### Week 1: Consolidate

- Collect all workshop outputs.
- Clean and standardize prompts.
- Merge duplicate use cases.
- Draft the Safe AI Usage Policy.
- Select AI champions.

### Week 2: Pilot

- Choose 1 use case per department.
- Test each use case with anonymized or low-risk content.
- Measure time spent before and after.
- Record errors, risks, and corrections.

### Week 3: Improve

- Refine prompts based on pilot results.
- Update document review checklist.
- Add missing safety rules.
- Create department quick guides.

### Week 4: Launch

- Approve final deliverables.
- Share prompt library and policy.
- Start monthly AI champions meeting.
- Decide which additional departments should join next.

## Ownership Recommendation

- HR owns training coordination and adoption tracking.
- Department managers own use-case approval.
- AI champions own prompt quality and feedback collection.
- Legal, IT, or Compliance should review the Safe AI Usage Policy before company-wide launch.
