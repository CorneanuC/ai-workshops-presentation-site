# Workshop 05: AI for HR and People Operations

Duration: 60 minutes

Audience: HR, Clients/Customer Service, Finance

## Purpose

Show how AI can support people-related work while protecting privacy, fairness, and human judgment.

## 60-Minute Agenda

- 0-5 min: Explain privacy and human approval in people-related tasks.
- 5-55 min: Five practical exercises.
- 55-60 min: Capture HR-related prompts and cross-department support needs.

## Exercise 1: Create an Onboarding Plan

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Create a 30-day onboarding plan for the role below. Include weekly goals, meetings, training topics, and manager check-ins.
```

HR:
- New production operator.

Clients/Customer Service:
- New customer service representative.

Finance:
- New finance assistant.

Output: 30-day onboarding plan.

## Exercise 2: Draft Training Communication

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Draft an internal training invitation. Include purpose, date placeholder, audience, expected benefit, and required action.
```

HR:
- AI basics for managers.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Customer complaint handling refresher.

Finance:
- Month-end submission rules.

Output: Training invitation draft.

## Exercise 3: Create Interview or Assessment Questions

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Create 8 practical interview or assessment questions for this role. Avoid discriminatory or personal questions.
```

HR:
- Team leader.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Customer service agent.

Finance:
- Accounts payable specialist.

Output: Question list with purpose for each question.

## Exercise 4: Summarize Feedback

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Summarize the anonymized feedback below into themes, positive signals, concerns, and recommended next steps.
```

HR:
- Training feedback.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Customer feedback themes.

Finance:
- Internal feedback about finance processes.

Output: Feedback summary.

## Exercise 5: Privacy Check

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Review this HR-related content and identify personal data, sensitive data, and information that should be removed before using AI.
```

HR:
- Employee case note.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Customer complaint with identifiers.

Finance:
- Expense note with personal details.

Output: Privacy removal checklist.
