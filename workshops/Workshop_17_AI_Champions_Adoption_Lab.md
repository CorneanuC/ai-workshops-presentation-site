# Workshop 17: AI Champions and Adoption Lab

Duration: 60 minutes

Audience: Department managers, HR, selected AI champions, IT, Compliance

## Purpose

Create the internal adoption model so the factory continues using AI safely and practically after the workshops.

## 60-Minute Agenda

- 0-5 min: Explain the AI champion role.
- 5-15 min: Select champions and backups.
- 15-25 min: Define responsibilities.
- 25-35 min: Create monthly review rhythm.
- 35-45 min: Define escalation rules.
- 45-55 min: Create 30-day adoption plan.
- 55-60 min: Confirm owners.

## Exercise 1: Select Champions

Time: 10 minutes

Selection criteria:

- trusted by colleagues
- understands daily department work
- careful with confidential data
- willing to test and improve prompts
- able to explain clearly

Task: Select 1 champion and 1 backup per department.

Output: AI champions list.

## Exercise 2: Define Champion Responsibilities

Time: 10 minutes

Responsibilities:

- collect useful prompts
- help colleagues use approved prompts
- report risks and mistakes
- update department prompt library
- collect productivity examples
- join monthly AI review meeting

Output: champion role description.

## Exercise 3: Create Monthly Review Rhythm

Time: 10 minutes

Agenda template:

```text
1. New use cases tested
2. Useful prompts added
3. Errors or risks found
4. Productivity examples collected
5. Policy questions
6. Next month priorities
```

Output: monthly AI review agenda.

## Exercise 4: Define Escalation Rules

Time: 10 minutes

Escalate when:

- personal data may be exposed
- customer or supplier confidentiality is involved
- AI output affects financial approval
- AI output affects employee decisions
- AI output makes legal, safety, or compliance claims

Output: escalation path.

## Exercise 5: Create 30-Day Adoption Plan

Time: 10 minutes

Template:

```text
Week 1: choose pilot and prepare sample data
Week 2: test approved prompts
Week 3: measure time saved and quality issues
Week 4: review results and decide next use case
```

Output: 30-day department adoption plan.

## Deliverable Contribution

This workshop contributes to:

- AI Champions Group
- Department Use-Case Map
- Before/After Productivity Examples
- Safe AI Usage Policy
