# Workshop 13: AI for SOPs, Work Instructions, and Training Materials

Duration: 60 minutes

Audience: Production, Quality, Maintenance, HR, Health and Safety, Logistics

## Purpose

Use AI to improve operational documents so they are clearer, easier to follow, and easier to train.

## 60-Minute Agenda

- 0-5 min: Explain AI support for SOPs and training materials.
- 5-15 min: Convert notes into an SOP.
- 15-25 min: Simplify technical wording.
- 25-35 min: Create a checklist.
- 35-45 min: Create training questions.
- 45-55 min: Review gaps and risks.
- 55-60 min: Save the improved artifact.

## Exercise 1: Convert Rough Notes into SOP Format

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Turn these rough notes into an SOP using this structure: purpose, scope, roles, required materials, step-by-step process, records, exceptions, escalation, owner. Identify missing information.
```

Examples:

- Production: line start-up process
- Quality: incoming inspection process
- Maintenance: machine restart after stoppage
- Logistics: goods receipt process
- HR: training attendance process

Output: SOP draft.

## Exercise 2: Simplify Work Instructions

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Rewrite this work instruction in simple language for shop-floor employees. Use short steps, active verbs, and clear warnings. Keep technical facts unchanged.
```

Output: simplified work instruction.

## Exercise 3: Create a Checklist from an SOP

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Create a practical checklist from this SOP. Include action, owner, evidence, and pass/fail status.
```

Output: checklist for daily use or audit readiness.

## Exercise 4: Create Training Materials

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Create a 15-minute training plan from this SOP. Include learning objective, trainer notes, demonstration activity, 5 quiz questions, and common mistakes.
```

Output: micro-training plan.

## Exercise 5: Review Gaps and Risks

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Review this SOP for missing safety warnings, unclear responsibilities, missing records, weak escalation steps, and assumptions that require human confirmation.
```

Output: SOP improvement list.
