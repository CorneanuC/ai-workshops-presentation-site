# Approved AI Tools and Usage Guide

## Purpose

This guide helps employees understand which AI tools they are allowed to use during the workshops and how to use them safely at work.

The company should review and approve this document before the training starts.

## Approved Tools

| Tool | Best Used For | Typical Workshop Use |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT Enterprise | Drafting, summarizing, reviewing, brainstorming, structured analysis, prompt practice | SOP improvement, document review, training material creation, use-case design |
| Microsoft 365 Copilot | Working inside Microsoft 365 apps such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams | Email drafts, meeting summaries, Excel insights, PowerPoint updates |
| Copilot in Word | Policies, procedures, SOPs, reports, work instructions | Rewrite SOPs, simplify work instructions, review clarity |
| Copilot in Excel | Tables, KPIs, formulas, trends, summaries | Analyze production KPIs, finance variance, training completion, complaint trends |
| Copilot in Outlook | Email drafting, rewriting, tone adjustment, summaries | Customer replies, supplier follow-ups, HR announcements |
| Copilot in Teams | Meeting summaries, action items, decisions, follow-ups | Department meetings, escalation meetings, shift review meetings |
| Copilot in PowerPoint | Presentations, training decks, management updates | KPI updates, training presentations, project summaries |
| SharePoint/OneDrive with Copilot | Finding, summarizing, and comparing internal documents | Policy search, procedure summary, document version comparison |

## Tool Selection Rules

Use ChatGPT Enterprise when:

- You need to draft, review, or improve text.
- You need help creating prompts.
- You are working with anonymized examples.
- You need structured thinking, such as tables, checklists, or plans.

Use Microsoft 365 Copilot when:

- The work is already inside Word, Excel, Outlook, Teams, or PowerPoint.
- You need to summarize a meeting or email thread.
- You need to work with internal Microsoft 365 files that the company allows Copilot to access.
- You need help with Excel formulas, charts, or summaries.

## Department Examples

### HR

Approved examples:

- Draft onboarding plans.
- Rewrite employee announcements.
- Summarize anonymized training feedback.
- Create training checklists.
- Improve policy wording.

Use with caution:

- Employee performance notes.
- Disciplinary cases.
- Personal employee data.
- Hiring decisions.

### Clients/Customer Service

Approved examples:

- Summarize customer complaints after removing sensitive details.
- Draft customer replies.
- Improve tone and clarity.
- Create escalation summaries.
- Identify missing information in a customer case.

Use with caution:

- Customer personal data.
- Contractual commitments.
- Refunds, penalties, or compensation promises.
- Legal or compliance claims.

### Finance

Approved examples:

- Explain budget variance text.
- Summarize anonymized invoice issues.
- Create Excel formula ideas.
- Draft payment follow-up emails.
- Prepare management summaries from verified figures.

Use with caution:

- Bank details.
- Payroll data.
- Supplier confidential pricing.
- Payment approvals.
- Financial decisions.

### Production

Approved examples:

- Turn shift notes into structured handovers.
- Summarize daily production issues.
- Draft work instructions.
- Create next-shift priority lists.
- Identify missing information in operational reports.

Use with caution:

- Confidential production volumes.
- Customer-specific production data.
- Safety-critical instructions without expert review.

### Quality

Approved examples:

- Improve nonconformity report clarity.
- Create audit checklists.
- Draft 5 Whys analysis.
- Summarize corrective actions.
- Check reports for missing facts.

Use with caution:

- Regulatory claims.
- Customer audit data.
- Final root cause conclusions without verification.
- Approved quality records.

### Maintenance

Approved examples:

- Summarize downtime notes.
- Create preventive maintenance checklists.
- Draft breakdown reports.
- Identify missing details in repair notes.
- Prepare handover notes.

Use with caution:

- Machine safety procedures.
- Critical technical instructions.
- Vendor confidential manuals.

## Data Usage Rules

| Data Type | AI Use | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Public or general information | Allowed | Safe for practice and general prompts |
| Internal process text | Allowed with care | Remove confidential details if not needed |
| Anonymized examples | Allowed | Recommended for workshops |
| Employee personal data | Restricted | Use only if approved by HR, Legal, and IT |
| Customer personal or confidential data | Restricted | Anonymize before use |
| Supplier contracts or prices | Restricted | Confirm company policy before use |
| Financial approvals or payment data | Restricted | AI may support drafting, but humans approve |
| Safety, legal, or compliance decisions | Restricted | AI can support review, not make final decisions |
| Passwords, access tokens, bank details | Not allowed | Never paste into AI tools |

## Green, Yellow, Red Rule

### Green: Usually Safe

- Generic prompts
- Public information
- Training examples
- Anonymized sample documents
- Drafting general communication
- Creating checklists or templates

### Yellow: Use With Care

- Internal procedures
- Internal reports
- Supplier or customer topics without sensitive details
- KPI summaries with anonymized data
- Drafts that need manager approval

### Red: Do Not Use Without Explicit Approval

- Personal employee data
- Customer confidential data
- Supplier bank details
- Payroll data
- Legal claims
- Safety-critical instructions
- Disciplinary decisions
- Payment approvals
- Passwords or access credentials

## Human Review Requirements

Employees must review AI output before using it.

Check:

- Are all facts supported by the input?
- Are numbers, dates, names, and departments correct?
- Did AI invent missing information?
- Is the tone appropriate?
- Are there unauthorized promises or commitments?
- Does the output expose confidential information?
- Is manager, HR, Legal, IT, Finance, or Compliance approval needed?

## Standard Safe Prompt

Use this prompt when reviewing documents:

```text
Review the text below for clarity, missing information, contradictions, risky wording, unsupported claims, and items requiring human verification. Do not invent facts. Do not change numbers. Return the answer as a table with: issue, why it matters, suggested improvement, and human verification needed.
```

## Standard Anonymization Prompt

Use this before sharing sensitive content with an AI tool:

```text
Identify personal, confidential, financial, customer, supplier, or sensitive information in the text below. Suggest an anonymized version that keeps the business meaning but removes sensitive details.
```

## Workshop Rule

During the workshops:

- Use only approved AI tools.
- Use anonymized examples whenever possible.
- Do not paste passwords, bank details, personal data, confidential customer data, or sensitive supplier data.
- Treat AI output as a draft.
- Keep a human responsible for final approval.

## Client Approval Checklist

Before Workshop 1, confirm:

- Which AI tools are approved.
- Which employees have access.
- Whether ChatGPT Enterprise is approved.
- Whether Microsoft 365 Copilot is approved.
- Whether employees may use real internal documents.
- Which data must be anonymized.
- Who approves the Safe AI Usage Policy.
- Who will be the AI champion in each department.
