# Workshop 04: AI for Document Accuracy and Double-Checking

Duration: 60 minutes

Audience: HR, Clients/Customer Service, Finance

## Purpose

Teach employees to use AI as a second reviewer for documents, while keeping human accountability for final approval.

## 60-Minute Agenda

- 0-5 min: Explain AI as reviewer, not final authority.
- 5-55 min: Five practical exercises.
- 55-60 min: Build the shared document review checklist.

## Exercise 1: Grammar and Clarity Review

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Review this document for grammar, spelling, unclear wording, and awkward phrasing. Do not change facts or numbers.
```

HR:
- Employee procedure or onboarding note.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Customer email template.

Finance:
- Invoice clarification message.

Output: Corrections list.

## Exercise 2: Missing Information Check

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Identify missing information that would prevent this document from being complete or actionable.
```

HR:
- Training request.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Complaint report.

Finance:
- Payment dispute note.

Output: Missing information table.

## Exercise 3: Contradiction Check

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Check this document for contradictions, inconsistent dates, inconsistent names, unclear ownership, and conflicting instructions.
```

HR:
- Shift training schedule.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Customer issue timeline.

Finance:
- Payment terms or invoice timeline.

Output: Contradiction and consistency report.

## Exercise 4: Checklist-Based Review

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Give AI a checklist and ask it to review against that checklist.

Prompt:

```text
Review this document against the checklist below. Return pass, fail, or needs review for each checklist item.
```

HR checklist:
- Audience is clear
- Date and location are included
- Required action is clear
- Contact person is included

Clients/Customer Service checklist:
- Customer issue is summarized
- Response is polite
- Next step is clear
- No unauthorized promise is made

Finance checklist:
- Amounts are unchanged
- Dates are included
- Cost center is included
- Approval need is clear

Output: Checklist review table.

## Exercise 5: Human Verification Log

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Identify what must still be checked by a person.

Prompt:

```text
Create a human verification log for this document. Include facts, numbers, dates, commitments, policy references, and decisions that require confirmation.
```

Output: Human verification log.
