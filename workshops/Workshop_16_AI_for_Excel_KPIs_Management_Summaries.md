# Workshop 16: AI for Excel, KPIs, and Management Summaries

Duration: 60 minutes

Audience: Finance, Production, Quality, Maintenance, Logistics, HR, Customer Service, Management

## Purpose

Use AI to understand KPI tables, create formulas, identify trends, and prepare concise management summaries.

## 60-Minute Agenda

- 0-5 min: Explain data safety and anonymization.
- 5-15 min: Ask for KPI analysis ideas.
- 15-25 min: Generate formulas or pivot suggestions.
- 25-35 min: Summarize trends.
- 35-45 min: Create a manager brief.
- 45-55 min: Verify calculations and assumptions.
- 55-60 min: Save data prompts.

## Exercise 1: Ask for KPI Analysis Ideas

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
I have an anonymized KPI table with these columns: [columns]. Suggest 5 useful analyses, what each analysis would show, and which chart or pivot table could help.
```

Factory examples:

- Production: output, scrap, downtime, shift, line
- Quality: defects, nonconformities, audit findings, defect type
- Maintenance: breakdowns, MTTR, preventive maintenance completion
- Logistics: on-time delivery, delay reason, warehouse accuracy
- HR: training completion, absence, overtime
- Customer Service: complaints, response time, satisfaction score
- Finance: overdue invoices, cost center, variance, supplier

Output: KPI analysis ideas.

## Exercise 2: Generate Formula and Pivot Suggestions

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Suggest Excel formulas and pivot table setup for this question. Explain each formula in simple language and mention what must be checked manually.
```

Output: formula or pivot guide.

## Exercise 3: Summarize Trends and Outliers

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Summarize this anonymized KPI table. Identify trends, outliers, possible causes, and questions for the manager. Do not change numbers.
```

Output: trend summary.

## Exercise 4: Create a Management Summary

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Turn this KPI analysis into a one-page management summary with: key message, evidence, impact, risks, recommended action, owner, and next review date.
```

Output: management summary.

## Exercise 5: Verify Calculations and Assumptions

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Review this analysis and list all calculations, assumptions, interpretations, and recommendations that require manual verification.
```

Output: data verification log.
