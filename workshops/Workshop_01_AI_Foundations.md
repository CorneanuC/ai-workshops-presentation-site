# Workshop 01: AI Foundations for Non-Technical Teams

Duration: 60 minutes

Audience: HR, Clients/Customer Service, Finance

## Purpose

Help employees understand what AI can do, what it cannot do, and where it can safely support daily work.

## 60-Minute Agenda

- 0-5 min: Explain AI as a work assistant for drafting, checking, summarizing, and organizing.
- 5-55 min: Five practical exercises.
- 55-60 min: Capture useful use cases, risks, and reusable prompts.

## Exercise 1: Identify Useful AI Tasks

Time: 10 minutes

Task: List 5 repetitive tasks where AI could save time.

HR:
- Drafting employee announcements
- Summarizing training feedback
- Creating onboarding checklists

Clients/Customer Service:
- Summarizing customer complaints
- Drafting professional replies
- Turning call notes into action items

Finance:
- Explaining budget variances
- Summarizing invoice issues
- Drafting payment follow-up emails

Output: A list of 5 AI use cases per department.

## Exercise 2: Separate Safe and Risky Uses

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Classify each AI use case as low risk, medium risk, or high risk.

HR:
- Low risk: Drafting a generic training invitation
- Medium risk: Summarizing anonymized survey results
- High risk: Making disciplinary recommendations

Clients/Customer Service:
- Low risk: Rewriting a response for clarity
- Medium risk: Summarizing a complaint with customer identifiers removed
- High risk: Promising refunds or legal commitments

Finance:
- Low risk: Explaining a formula
- Medium risk: Summarizing anonymized cost data
- High risk: Approving payments or financial decisions

Output: A first draft of the AI risk map.

## Exercise 3: Ask AI for a Simple Summary

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Summarize the text below in 5 bullet points. Keep only the facts provided. Do not invent missing information.
```

HR:
- Use a sample training feedback note.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Use a sample customer complaint note.

Finance:
- Use a sample monthly spending explanation.

Output: A short summary and a note about what the AI missed or improved.

## Exercise 4: Check AI Limitations

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Give AI an incomplete note and ask it what information is missing.

Prompt:

```text
Review this note and identify missing information, unclear wording, and assumptions that need human confirmation.
```

HR:
- Incomplete employee onboarding request.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Incomplete service complaint.

Finance:
- Incomplete invoice dispute.

Output: A list of missing facts that must be confirmed by a person.

## Exercise 5: Build a Personal AI Rule

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Each participant writes 3 rules for using AI safely in their work.

Example:

```text
I will not paste personal employee data into AI tools.
I will verify facts before sending AI-assisted documents.
I will use AI as a reviewer, not as the final decision-maker.
```

Output: Department safety rules to reuse in the Safe AI Usage Policy.

## Deliverable Contribution

This workshop contributes to:

- Department Use-Case Map
- Safe AI Usage Policy
- AI risk categories
