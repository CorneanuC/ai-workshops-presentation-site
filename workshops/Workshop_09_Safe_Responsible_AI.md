# Workshop 09: Safe and Responsible AI Use

Duration: 60 minutes

Audience: HR, Clients/Customer Service, Finance

## Purpose

Create common rules for responsible AI use, including privacy, verification, confidentiality, bias, and human accountability.

## 60-Minute Agenda

- 0-5 min: Explain why AI safety is a business requirement.
- 5-55 min: Five practical exercises.
- 55-60 min: Finalize policy inputs.

## Exercise 1: Data Classification

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Classify examples as green, yellow, or red.

Definitions:

- Green: safe general content
- Yellow: internal content after removing sensitive data
- Red: confidential, personal, legal, financial, or sensitive data that should not be pasted into public AI tools

HR:
- Employee names, performance notes, disciplinary cases

Clients/Customer Service:
- Customer names, contract terms, complaint details

Finance:
- Supplier bank data, salary data, payment approvals

Output: Data classification examples.

## Exercise 2: Remove Sensitive Data

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Identify sensitive or confidential information in this text and suggest a safe anonymized version.
```

HR:
- Employee case note.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Customer complaint.

Finance:
- Supplier invoice note.

Output: Anonymized version.

## Exercise 3: Hallucination Check

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Review this AI-generated answer. Identify claims that are unsupported, too confident, or need source verification.
```

HR:
- AI-generated policy explanation.

Clients/Customer Service:
- AI-generated customer commitment.

Finance:
- AI-generated accounting explanation.

Output: Unsupported claims list.

## Exercise 4: Bias and Fairness Review

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Review this text for biased, unfair, or inappropriate wording. Suggest neutral alternatives.
```

HR:
- Hiring or performance wording.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Customer complaint categorization.

Finance:
- Internal comment about delayed approvals.

Output: Neutral wording suggestions.

## Exercise 5: Create Department AI Rules

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Write 5 department-specific AI rules.

HR:
- Rules for employee data, hiring, feedback, and training.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Rules for customer commitments, complaints, and personal data.

Finance:
- Rules for financial data, approvals, supplier information, and calculations.

Output: Department rules for the Safe AI Usage Policy.

## Deliverable Contribution

This workshop contributes to:

- Safe AI Usage Policy
- Document Review Checklist
- AI Champions Group
