# Workshop 03: AI for Emails, Reports, and Daily Communication

Duration: 60 minutes

Audience: HR, Clients/Customer Service, Finance

## Purpose

Help employees use AI to write clearer messages, adapt tone, summarize notes, and prepare communication for different audiences.

## 60-Minute Agenda

- 0-5 min: Explain audience, tone, clarity, and factual accuracy.
- 5-55 min: Five practical exercises.
- 55-60 min: Save useful examples in the prompt library.

## Exercise 1: Rewrite for Clarity

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Rewrite a long or unclear message into a clear version.

Prompt:

```text
Rewrite this message in clear, simple business language. Keep the meaning unchanged and flag any unclear information.
```

HR:
- Rewrite a training announcement.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Rewrite a reply to a delayed delivery complaint.

Finance:
- Rewrite an explanation of missing invoice information.

Output: One clear message.

## Exercise 2: Change the Tone

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Create 3 tone versions of the same message.

Prompt:

```text
Create three versions of this message: formal, friendly, and short/direct. Keep facts unchanged.
```

HR:
- Employee reminder about mandatory training.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Customer update about a service issue.

Finance:
- Reminder to submit missing receipts.

Output: Three tone versions and the best choice.

## Exercise 3: Summarize Meeting Notes

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Turn rough notes into a structured summary.

Prompt:

```text
Turn these notes into a meeting summary with decisions, action items, owners, deadlines, and open questions.
```

HR:
- Training planning meeting.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Complaint escalation meeting.

Finance:
- Month-end review meeting.

Output: Meeting summary with action items.

## Exercise 4: Translate and Simplify

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Convert technical or complex wording into simpler language.

Prompt:

```text
Rewrite this text for employees who are not specialists. Use short sentences and avoid jargon.
```

HR:
- Policy update.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Service process explanation.

Finance:
- Budget rule explanation.

Output: Simple-language version.

## Exercise 5: Final Accuracy Check

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Ask AI to check the final communication before sending.

Prompt:

```text
Review this message before sending. Check clarity, tone, missing context, contradictions, and statements that need verification.
```

HR:
- Employee communication.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Customer response.

Finance:
- Finance reminder or report comment.

Output: Final review notes and revised message.
