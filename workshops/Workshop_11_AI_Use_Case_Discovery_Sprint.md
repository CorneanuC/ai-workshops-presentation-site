# Workshop 11: AI Use-Case Discovery Sprint

Duration: 60 minutes

Audience: All factory departments

## Purpose

Help each department identify practical AI use cases and select 2 high-value, low-risk pilots.

## 60-Minute Agenda

- 0-5 min: Explain what makes a good AI use case.
- 5-15 min: Map repetitive tasks.
- 15-25 min: Score tasks by value and risk.
- 25-40 min: Select pilot use cases.
- 40-55 min: Define workflow inputs, outputs, owner, and success metric.
- 55-60 min: Save the use cases into the Department Use-Case Map.

## Exercise 1: List 10 Repetitive Tasks

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Each department lists 10 tasks that involve writing, summarizing, checking, analyzing, or formatting.

Examples:

- HR: onboarding plans, training reminders, employee FAQs
- Production: shift notes, downtime summaries, daily production reports
- Quality: nonconformity reports, audit preparation, corrective action drafts
- Maintenance: breakdown reports, preventive maintenance checklists
- Logistics: delivery delay summaries, warehouse issue logs
- Procurement: supplier comparison notes, negotiation emails
- Customer Service: complaint summaries, customer reply drafts
- Finance: invoice issue summaries, variance explanations

Output: 10 possible AI use cases per department.

## Exercise 2: Score Value and Risk

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Score each task from 1 to 5.

Value score:
- Time saved
- Quality improved
- Frequency of task
- Pain level

Risk score:
- Confidential data involved
- Impact of wrong output
- Need for human approval
- Legal, financial, or customer sensitivity

Output: Value/risk score table.

## Exercise 3: Select 2 Pilot Use Cases

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Pick 2 use cases with high value and manageable risk.

Selection rule:

```text
Choose tasks that are frequent, document-heavy, easy to review, and do not require confidential data in the AI tool.
```

Output: 2 pilot use cases per department.

## Exercise 4: Define the AI Workflow

Time: 15 minutes

Template:

```text
Use case:

Input:
AI task:
Output:
Human review:
Risk level:
Owner:
Success metric:
```

Output: One complete workflow for the first pilot.

## Exercise 5: Define Success Measures

Time: 10 minutes

Examples:

- Reduce report preparation from 30 minutes to 15 minutes.
- Reduce missing fields in forms by 50%.
- Improve email response consistency.
- Reduce rework after manager review.
- Create weekly KPI summaries faster.

Output: 1-2 measurable success indicators per pilot.
