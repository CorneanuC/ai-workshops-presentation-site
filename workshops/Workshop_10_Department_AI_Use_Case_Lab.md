# Workshop 10: Department AI Use-Case Lab

Duration: 60 minutes

Audience: HR, Clients/Customer Service, Finance

## Purpose

Turn learning into practical department workflows, prompt libraries, safety rules, and productivity examples.

## 60-Minute Agenda

- 0-5 min: Explain final lab outputs.
- 5-55 min: Five practical exercises.
- 55-60 min: Confirm owners and next steps.

## Exercise 1: Select Top Use Cases

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Select 3 high-value AI use cases for the department.

Selection criteria:

- Saves time
- Improves accuracy
- Low or manageable risk
- Can be tested quickly
- Has a clear owner

HR:
- Onboarding plan assistant
- Training feedback summarizer
- Employee communication reviewer

Clients/Customer Service:
- Complaint summary assistant
- Customer reply reviewer
- Ticket trend summarizer

Finance:
- Invoice issue summarizer
- Variance explanation assistant
- Excel formula helper

Output: Top 3 use cases per department.

## Exercise 2: Build the Workflow

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Define the workflow for one use case.

Template:

```text
Trigger:
Input:
AI prompt:
Human review:
Final output:
Owner:
Risk controls:
```

Output: One complete AI workflow.

## Exercise 3: Finalize 5 Reusable Prompts

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Select or improve 5 prompts from previous workshops.

HR:
- Communication review
- Onboarding plan
- Training summary
- Feedback analysis
- Privacy check

Clients/Customer Service:
- Complaint summary
- Reply improvement
- Escalation summary
- Tone adjustment
- Commitment check

Finance:
- Variance explanation
- Invoice issue summary
- Formula request
- Manager brief
- Calculation verification

Output: 5 final prompts per department.

## Exercise 4: Define Success Measures

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Choose 2-3 measures for the use case.

Examples:

- Time saved per document
- Fewer review corrections
- Faster response time
- Fewer missing fields
- Better consistency

Output: Success measures and baseline method.

## Exercise 5: Create 30-Day Adoption Plan

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Create a 30-day plan for testing AI in the department.

Prompt:

```text
Create a 30-day adoption plan for this AI use case. Include weekly actions, owner, training need, safety check, success measure, and final review.
```

Output: 30-day department adoption plan.
