# Workshop 06: AI for Operations and Productivity

Duration: 60 minutes

Audience: HR, Clients/Customer Service, Finance

## Purpose

Use AI to make daily workflows faster, clearer, and easier to follow across factory support departments.

## 60-Minute Agenda

- 0-5 min: Explain process improvement and productivity use cases.
- 5-55 min: Five practical exercises.
- 55-60 min: Capture productivity examples.

## Exercise 1: Turn Notes into a Process

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Turn these rough notes into a step-by-step process. Identify missing steps and unclear responsibilities.
```

HR:
- Employee onboarding steps.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Customer complaint handling steps.

Finance:
- Invoice approval steps.

Output: Step-by-step process.

## Exercise 2: Create a Standard Operating Procedure Draft

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Create a simple SOP draft using this structure: purpose, scope, roles, steps, exceptions, records, owner.
```

HR:
- Training attendance tracking.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Complaint escalation.

Finance:
- Missing invoice data handling.

Output: SOP draft.

## Exercise 3: Find Bottlenecks

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Review this process and identify bottlenecks, duplicated work, unclear ownership, and possible improvements.
```

HR:
- Manual training registration.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Repeated customer follow-ups.

Finance:
- Delayed invoice approvals.

Output: Bottleneck list.

## Exercise 4: Create a Daily Checklist

Time: 10 minutes

Prompt:

```text
Create a practical daily checklist for this role. Keep it short and focused on actions that prevent errors.
```

HR:
- HR coordinator.

Clients/Customer Service:
- Customer service agent.

Finance:
- Accounts payable clerk.

Output: Daily checklist.

## Exercise 5: Estimate Time Saved

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Compare old workflow and AI-assisted workflow.

Prompt:

```text
Compare the current workflow and AI-assisted workflow. Estimate where time is saved, what risks remain, and what still requires human approval.
```

Output: Before/after productivity example.
